COMPANY HR POLICY — INDIA
Version 2024 | Applicable to all employees in India

========================================================
1. WORKING HOURS
========================================================
Standard working hours are 9 hours per day, 5 days a week (Monday to Friday).
Total working hours per week: 45 hours.
Employees are entitled to a 1-hour lunch break which is not counted in working hours.
Overtime is applicable beyond 45 hours per week and is paid at 2x the normal hourly rate.
Night shifts (10 PM to 6 AM) attract an additional night shift allowance of 20% of basic salary.

========================================================
2. LEAVE POLICY
========================================================
Annual Leave (Earned Leave):
- Employees are entitled to 21 days of earned leave per year.
- Leave accrues at 1.75 days per month.
- Maximum carry-forward: 30 days to the next year.
- Leave encashment is allowed for up to 15 days at the time of resignation or retirement.

Sick Leave:
- 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.
- Medical certificate required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
- Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

Casual Leave:
- 8 days of casual leave per calendar year.
- Cannot be combined with earned leave or sick leave.
- Cannot be carried forward.

Maternity Leave:
- Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks (182 days) of paid maternity leave.
- Applicable for first two surviving children.
- For third child onwards: 12 weeks of maternity leave.
- Adoption leave: 12 weeks for adopting a child below 3 months.

Paternity Leave:
- Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave.
- Must be availed within 6 months of the child's birth.

Public Holidays:
- 10 national and regional public holidays per year.
- Republic Day (26 Jan), Independence Day (15 Aug), Gandhi Jayanti (2 Oct) are mandatory.
- Remaining 7 holidays are decided by the company based on state and region.

========================================================
3. PROBATION PERIOD
========================================================
Standard probation period is 6 months from the date of joining.
During probation, the employee is on a 30-day notice period.
The probation period may be extended by a maximum of 3 months based on performance.
Employees on probation are not eligible for promotion or role change.
All leave benefits apply during probation except earned leave encashment.

========================================================
4. NOTICE PERIOD
========================================================
Post-confirmation notice period: 60 days (2 months).
Senior roles (Manager and above): 90 days (3 months).
Notice period can be waived off by paying salary in lieu of notice.
Gardening leave can be applied during the notice period at the company's discretion.

========================================================
5. GRATUITY
========================================================
Employees completing 5 or more years of continuous service are entitled to gratuity.
Gratuity is calculated as: (Last drawn salary x 15 x Years of service) / 26.
Maximum gratuity payable as per the Payment of Gratuity Act: INR 20,00,000.
Gratuity is paid within 30 days of leaving the organisation.

========================================================
6. PROVIDENT FUND (PF)
========================================================
Both employer and employee contribute 12% of basic salary to PF.
Employer contribution split: 3.67% to EPF and 8.33% to EPS (Employee Pension Scheme).
Employees can withdraw PF after 2 months of unemployment or on retirement.
PF is managed under the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO).

========================================================
7. PERFORMANCE REVIEW
========================================================
Performance appraisals are conducted annually in April.
Mid-year review is conducted in October for course correction.
Rating scale: Outstanding (5), Exceeds Expectations (4), Meets Expectations (3),
              Needs Improvement (2), Unsatisfactory (1).
Salary increments are based on the appraisal rating and company performance.
Employees rated Unsatisfactory for two consecutive years may face termination.

========================================================
8. CODE OF CONDUCT
========================================================
Employees must maintain confidentiality of company data at all times.
Use of company assets for personal purposes is prohibited.
Workplace harassment of any form (verbal, physical, digital) is grounds for immediate termination.
Employees must disclose any conflict of interest to the HR department.
Social media usage: Employees must not post confidential or derogatory content about the company.

========================================================
9. TERMINATION POLICY
========================================================
Termination for cause (misconduct, fraud): No notice period, immediate effect.
Termination without cause: Full notice period or payment in lieu.
All company assets must be returned on the last working day.
Full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day.
Experience letter and relieving letter issued within 15 days of full and final settlement.
