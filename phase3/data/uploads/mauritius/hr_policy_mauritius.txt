- Intent Tag: disconnect [cite: 47]

4. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT & TERMINATION
========================================================
Unlike the US at-will default, termination and structural redundancies in Mauritius require objective justification under the Workers’ Rights Act 2019[cite: 47].

Written Employment Contract (WRA-MU-EN-02):
- Intent Tag: written_contract [cite: 47]
- Policy: Yes, an employee is entitled to a written employment contract[cite: 47]. A written contract must specify duties, remuneration, hours, leave and termination conditions[cite: 47]. Lack of written form does not remove worker rights[cite: 47].
- Category: Employment Contract | Sensitivity: Medium | User Type: Employee [cite: 47]
- Internal Reference: POL-CONTRACT | Slug: wra-mu-en-02-am-i-entitled-to-a-written-employment-contract [cite: 47]

Economic Redundancy (WRA-MU-EN-10):
- Intent Tag: redundancy [cite: 47]
- Policy: What steps apply for an economic redundancy? Information/consultation, objective criteria and exploration of alternatives (redeployment) are strictly required[cite: 47].
- Category: Termination | Sensitivity: High | User Type: Manager [cite: 47]
- Internal Reference: POL-REDUNDANCY | Slug: wra-mu-en-10-what-steps-apply-for-an-economic-redundancy [cite: 47]
