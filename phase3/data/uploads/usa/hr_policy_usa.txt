COMPANY HR POLICY — UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
Version 2024 | Applicable to all US-based employees

========================================================
1. WORKING HOURS
========================================================
Standard working hours are 40 hours per week (8 hours/day, Monday to Friday).
The United States follows the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) for wage and hour rules.
Non-exempt employees must be paid 1.5x their regular rate for hours over 40 per week (overtime).
Exempt employees (salaried professionals) are not entitled to overtime pay.
Meal and rest breaks vary by state. Generally: 30-min unpaid meal break per 8-hour shift.

========================================================
2. LEAVE POLICY
========================================================
Paid Time Off (PTO):
- Employees receive 15 days PTO per year for the first 3 years.
- 20 days PTO per year after 3 years of service.
- 25 days PTO per year after 7 years of service.
- PTO can be used for vacation, personal days, or illness.
- Unused PTO: Up to 5 days can be rolled over to the next calendar year.

Sick Leave:
- Included within PTO. Employees in states with mandatory sick leave laws
  (e.g., California, New York) receive additional protections.
- California: 5 days (40 hours) of paid sick leave per year.
- New York: 56 hours paid sick leave for companies with 100+ employees.

Maternity & Paternity Leave (FMLA):
- Federal FMLA provides 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave for birth/adoption.
- Eligibility: Must have worked for the company for 12+ months and 1,250+ hours.
- Company Policy (enhanced): 8 weeks PAID maternity leave for birth mothers.
- Company Policy (enhanced): 4 weeks PAID paternity/adoption leave for all parents.
- State-specific paid family leave may provide additional benefits (California, NY, NJ, etc.)

Public Holidays:
- 11 federal holidays observed: New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents Day,
  Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day (4th July), Labor Day,
  Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day.
- If a holiday falls on a weekend, the nearest weekday is observed.

Bereavement Leave:
- 3 days paid leave for immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- 1 day paid leave for extended family (grandparents, in-laws).

========================================================
3. PROBATION PERIOD
========================================================
All new employees serve a 90-day introductory period.
During this period, employment is at-will and either party may terminate without notice.
Health insurance benefits begin on Day 1 of employment (company policy).
401(k) enrollment is available after 90 days of employment.
Performance check-in at 30, 60, and 90 days with direct manager.

========================================================
4. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT & TERMINATION
========================================================
Employment in the US is at-will by default. Either party can terminate at any time.
Company standard courtesy notice: 2 weeks for employees, 2 weeks for company.
Involuntary termination: Severance of 1 week pay per year of service (minimum 2 weeks).
WARN Act: Companies with 100+ employees must give 60 days notice for mass layoffs.
Final paycheck: Issued on the last day of employment (varies by state law).
COBRA: Terminated employees may continue health insurance for up to 18 months at own cost.

========================================================
5. COMPENSATION & BENEFITS
========================================================
Pay Schedule: Bi-weekly (every two weeks, 26 pay periods per year).
Health Insurance: Company covers 80% of employee premium and 70% of dependent premium.
Dental & Vision: Company covers 80% of employee premium.
401(k) Retirement Plan: Company matches 4% of salary (100% match up to 4% contribution).
Life Insurance: 2x annual salary provided at no cost to employee.
HSA/FSA: Available for employees enrolled in high-deductible health plans.
Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Free counselling and mental health support.

========================================================
6. PERFORMANCE REVIEW
========================================================
Annual performance reviews conducted in December.
Mid-year check-in in June.
Rating scale: Exceptional, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations,
              Below Expectations, Unsatisfactory.
Merit increases effective January 1 based on review outcome.
Performance Improvement Plan (PIP): Issued for Below Expectations rating.
PIP duration: 30-90 days. Failure to meet PIP goals may result in termination.

========================================================
7. ANTI-DISCRIMINATION & HARASSMENT POLICY
========================================================
The company complies fully with Title VII, ADA, ADEA, and all applicable federal/state laws.
Discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability,
or genetic information is strictly prohibited.
All harassment complaints must be reported to HR within 30 days of the incident.
Investigations are completed within 30 business days.
Retaliation against employees who report harassment is a terminable offense.

========================================================
8. REMOTE WORK POLICY
========================================================
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week (hybrid model).
Fully remote roles are designated at the time of offer.
Remote employees must be available during core hours: 10 AM to 3 PM local time.
Company provides: Laptop, monitor, and $500 one-time home office stipend.
Internet reimbursement: Up to $50/month for home internet expenses.
